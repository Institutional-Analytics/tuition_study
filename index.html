Today each student pays part of the tuition in US dollars and the rest in Lebanese lira at a fixed rate. From the whole bill is paid in US dollars. Prices per credit stay the same, so a student's increase is exactly the part they used to pay in lira, now paid in dollars. Everything below is in US dollars.
